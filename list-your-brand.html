Frequently asked questions

How much does brand membership cost?

Membership is an annual package. Pricing depends on the plan and number of industries. Contact us for current pricing.

What documents do I need to register?

Company registration certificate, tax or VAT registration, your logo and authorised contact details. Brand images and videos are optional.

How long does verification take?

Our team reviews each application and confirms by email once your profile is approved.

Leaderboard and billboard banners, 300 × 250 display ads, sponsored features, sponsored videos, newsletter sponsorship, forum debate sponsorship and industry partner placements.
