Industrial technology in UAE

The United Arab Emirates is a global hub for trade, energy and large-scale construction, and a leading host of international exhibitions. Future Markets Technology connects UAE projects and companies with technology providers worldwide.

Key industries in UAE

EnergyThe UAE combines a major oil and gas industry with large-scale solar and clean energy investment. Solar parks, nuclear power and clean energy investment sit alongside a large hydrocarbon industry.
ConstructionLandmark developments and infrastructure projects make the UAE a showcase for construction technology. Projects use BIM, prefabrication and smart building systems at scale.
Water & desalinationDesalination is central to the UAE's water supply. Utilities invest in efficient reverse osmosis plants and water storage.
HealthcareRapidly developing healthcare systems in Dubai and Abu Dhabi are adopting digital health. New hospitals and digital health platforms are expanding quickly.

Technology focus in UAE

UAE economic strategies prioritise diversification, advanced industry, AI and sustainability. Dubai and Abu Dhabi host many of the region's largest trade exhibitions, making the country a natural meeting point for global industry.

Opportunities for technology providers in UAE

The UAE invests at large scale in energy, construction, water and healthcare, and actively seeks advanced technology partners. Free zones, modern infrastructure and its position between Europe, Asia and Africa make it a natural regional hub. Dubai and Abu Dhabi host many of the region's largest exhibitions, making it easy to meet buyers from across the Gulf. Local partnerships and a regional presence help win contracts.
