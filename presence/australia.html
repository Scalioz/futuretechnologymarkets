Industrial technology in Australia

Australia is one of the world's leading mining nations and a global leader in mining equipment, technology and services (METS). Future Markets Technology connects Australian innovators with mining, energy and water markets worldwide.

Key industries in Australia

Mining & critical mineralsAustralia is a leading producer of iron ore, gold and lithium, with major regions such as the Pilbara and Kalgoorlie in Western Australia. Mining remains one of the country's most important export industries and a major buyer of technology.
Mining technology (METS)Australian mines were early adopters of autonomous haulage and remote operations centres. Remote operations centres in cities such as Perth manage mines thousands of kilometres away.
WaterFrequent droughts have made Australia a leader in water efficiency, recycling and desalination. Utilities invest in desalination, water recycling and smart network management.
Renewable energyStrong solar resources and growing storage deployments are reshaping Australia's energy mix. Rooftop solar adoption is among the highest in the world, and grid-scale batteries are expanding.

Technology focus in Australia

Australian companies export mining know-how to operations worldwide and are building capabilities in critical minerals processing, battery materials and renewable hydrogen.

Opportunities for technology providers in Australia

Australia's mining, energy and water sectors are early adopters of technology that improves safety and productivity in remote locations. Investment in critical minerals, battery materials and renewable hydrogen is creating new demand. Australian METS companies also export their solutions to mining regions around the world. Proven safety performance and remote support capability are essential.
