Industrial technology in USA

The United States is the world's largest market for healthcare and life sciences and a global leader in AI, software and advanced manufacturing. Future Markets Technology connects American technology brands with industrial buyers worldwide.

Key industries in USA

Healthcare & medtechThe US is home to leading medical device, digital health and life sciences clusters around Boston, the San Francisco Bay Area, Minneapolis and San Diego. Hospitals and health systems are major buyers of AI diagnostics, remote monitoring and hospital automation.
Pharma & biotechAmerican companies lead in biologics, cell and gene therapy and pharmaceutical R&D. Biologics, cell and gene therapy and advanced manufacturing are attracting large investments in new facilities.
EnergyThe US combines a large oil and gas industry with rapid growth in solar, wind, storage and grid technology. Grid modernisation, storage and clean energy manufacturing are growing with strong policy support.
Specialty chemicalsA broad chemical industry, including major clusters on the Gulf Coast, supports manufacturing across sectors. Producers are investing in process efficiency, digitalisation and lower-carbon production.

Technology focus in USA

US industry is investing in reshoring, semiconductor and battery manufacturing, AI across operations and grid modernisation. Strong venture capital and university research keep a steady flow of new technology companies entering industrial markets.

Opportunities for technology providers in USA

The US market rewards proven technology with large contracts and long-term relationships. Federal programmes support clean energy, semiconductors, batteries and infrastructure, creating demand for equipment and software across many industries. Health systems, life sciences companies and utilities are major buyers of digital and automation technology. Regulatory compliance and strong references are essential.
