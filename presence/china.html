Industrial technology in China

China is the world's largest manufacturer and a leading producer of renewable energy equipment, textiles, chemicals and construction materials. Future Markets Technology connects Chinese technology brands with global buyers, and international companies with Chinese industry.

Key industries in China

Renewable energy manufacturingChina produces a large share of the world's solar panels, batteries and wind components. Chinese manufacturers lead in solar, battery and electric vehicle supply chains.
Textiles & apparelChina is the world's largest textile and apparel producer and exporter. Producers are investing in automation, recycled fibres and digital supply chains.
ChemicalsChina has the world's largest chemical industry by sales. Chemical parks are modernising with stricter safety and environmental standards.
ConstructionLarge infrastructure programmes have made China a major user of construction technology. Contractors use prefabrication, BIM and large-scale equipment on major projects.

Technology focus in China

Chinese industry is investing heavily in automation, smart manufacturing, electric mobility and clean energy supply chains.

Opportunities for technology providers in China

China's manufacturers are rapidly adopting automation, smart manufacturing and clean energy technology, and its suppliers serve buyers worldwide. International companies find opportunities in specialised equipment, software and high-performance materials, while Chinese companies seek global visibility and partners. Local regulations, certification and strong partners are essential for success in the Chinese market.
