Our global presence

Industry coverage, a verified brand network and partnerships in 14 countries across Asia, Europe, the Middle East, North America and Oceania.

India

India is one of the world's fastest-growing major economies and a manufacturing powerhouse in pharmaceuticals, textiles, food processing and engineering. Future Markets Technology connects Indian technology brands with global buyers, and global innovators with Indian industry.

USA

The United States is the world's largest market for healthcare and life sciences and a global leader in AI, software and advanced manufacturing. Future Markets Technology connects American technology brands with industrial buyers worldwide.

Germany

Germany is Europe's largest economy and the home of Industrie 4.0. Its engineering, chemicals, machinery and medical technology companies set global standards. Future Markets Technology connects German technology brands with growth markets worldwide.

Italy

Italy combines design excellence with world-class industrial machinery. Its packaging, textile and food technology companies supply manufacturers across the globe. Future Markets Technology showcases Italian innovation to international buyers.

Singapore

Singapore is Southeast Asia's business hub and a global reference for water technology, chemicals and biomedical sciences. Future Markets Technology connects Singapore-based innovators with industry across Asia and beyond.

Australia

Australia is one of the world's leading mining nations and a global leader in mining equipment, technology and services (METS). Future Markets Technology connects Australian innovators with mining, energy and water markets worldwide.

France

France is a major European industrial economy with strengths in pharmaceuticals, energy, infrastructure and food. Future Markets Technology connects French technology brands with buyers across our global network.

Sweden

Sweden combines a strong engineering tradition with ambitious climate goals. Its mining equipment, life sciences and clean industry companies are known worldwide. Future Markets Technology connects Swedish innovators with global markets.

Denmark

Denmark pioneered modern wind power and remains a global leader in offshore wind. It is also home to strong life sciences, water and food technology companies. Future Markets Technology connects Danish innovators with the world.

UAE

The United Arab Emirates is a global hub for trade, energy and large-scale construction, and a leading host of international exhibitions. Future Markets Technology connects UAE projects and companies with technology providers worldwide.

UK

The United Kingdom is a global centre for life sciences, engineering and offshore wind, with London as a leading hub for finance and business services. Future Markets Technology connects UK innovators with industry worldwide.

Canada

Canada is a global mining and energy powerhouse with a growing clean technology sector. Future Markets Technology connects Canadian innovators with buyers and partners worldwide.

China

China is the world's largest manufacturer and a leading producer of renewable energy equipment, textiles, chemicals and construction materials. Future Markets Technology connects Chinese technology brands with global buyers, and international companies with Chinese industry.

Russia

Russia has one of the world's largest resource industries, including oil, gas, metals, mining and fertilisers. Future Markets Technology provides industry coverage and verified company profiles, in line with applicable international trade regulations.

Future Markets Technology is the global platform for industrial technology: exclusive industry news, verified brands, leaders and events across ten high-growth industries.
