Key industries in Russia

EnergyRussia is one of the world's largest producers of oil and natural gas. The sector relies on large-scale extraction, pipelines and processing infrastructure.
Mining & metalsRussia is a major producer of nickel, palladium, aluminium and other metals. Operations span some of the world's largest mining regions, including in the Arctic.
Chemicals & fertilisersRussia is a leading global producer of fertilisers. Chemical complexes supply agricultural and industrial markets.
ConstructionLarge infrastructure and housing programmes drive construction activity. Building programmes cover housing, transport and energy infrastructure.

Technology focus in Russia

Russian industry is focused on resource processing, domestic manufacturing and automation of large industrial sites.
