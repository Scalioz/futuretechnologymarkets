Industrial technology in Sweden

Sweden combines a strong engineering tradition with ambitious climate goals. Its mining equipment, life sciences and clean industry companies are known worldwide. Future Markets Technology connects Swedish innovators with global markets.

Key industries in Sweden

Mining & mining equipmentSweden is a major European iron ore producer, with mining in the far north, and Swedish firms supply mining equipment globally.
Life sciencesThe Stockholm-Uppsala region and the Öresund region host strong pharma and medtech clusters.
Clean industrySweden is home to pioneering fossil-free steel and battery manufacturing initiatives.
Construction & timberSwedish companies are known for modular and timber-based construction.

Technology focus in Sweden

Swedish industry focuses on decarbonising heavy industry, electrification and digitalisation, supported by abundant hydro and wind power.
