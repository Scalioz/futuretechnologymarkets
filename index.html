Frequently asked questions

What is Future Markets Technology?

Future Markets Technology is a global B2B portal for industrial technology. It combines exclusive industry news, a searchable database of verified brands, a Leaders Forum for senior executives and events across ten industries.

Which industries does the portal cover?

Pharma, healthcare, textiles and apparel, food processing, packaging, mining and minerals, specialty chemicals, water, construction and energy.

Which countries does the portal cover?

India, USA, Germany, Italy, Singapore, Australia, France, Sweden, Denmark, UAE, UK, Canada, China and Russia.

How does a brand get listed?

Choose an annual membership, then either self-register online with your documents or email them to info@futuretechmarkets.com. Every brand is verified before its profile goes live.

Can I advertise on the portal?

Yes. Display advertising, sponsored features, newsletter sponsorships and industry partner placements are available. Contact us for the media kit.
