A quarterly magazine covering technology across all ten industries, and an industry journal with deeper technical analysis.

Each issue combines in-depth features on the technologies changing each industry, interviews with members of the Leaders Forum, country reports from our 14 markets and coverage of brand launches and major expos. The industry journal goes deeper into technical topics such as process automation, critical minerals processing and water reuse, written for engineers and technical managers.

The magazine is published in print for distribution at major trade shows and in digital editions for readers worldwide. Members receive featured coverage, and advertisers can book print and digital placements.
