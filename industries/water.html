Water & Wastewater Technology

Water security is one of the defining challenges of this century. Future Markets Technology brings together the water treatment, desalination, reuse and digital water companies helping cities and industries do more with every drop.

Key water technologies we cover

DesalinationReverse osmosis and thermal plants turn seawater and brackish water into drinking and process water. Energy recovery devices and better membranes have cut energy use substantially. Desalination is essential in many water-scarce regions.
Water reuse & recyclingAdvanced treatment, including membrane filtration and disinfection, turns wastewater into a reliable resource for industry, irrigation and even drinking water. Reuse reduces pressure on rivers and aquifers. Industrial sites can recycle much of their own process water.
Smart meteringConnected meters report consumption in near real time to utilities and customers. Utilities detect leaks, theft and billing errors quickly, and customers can track and reduce their use. Smart metering is the foundation of a digital water network.
AI leak detectionAcoustic sensors, pressure monitoring and analytics locate leaks in pipe networks before they become bursts. Utilities can reduce non-revenue water and repair costs. AI models prioritise which pipes to replace first.
Membrane technologyMicrofiltration, ultrafiltration, nanofiltration and reverse osmosis membranes remove particles, bacteria and dissolved salts. Newer membranes need less energy and resist fouling better. Membranes are used in drinking water, wastewater and industrial treatment.
Industrial water treatmentTreatment systems let factories, power plants and mines reduce intake, clean process water and meet discharge rules. Zero liquid discharge systems recover almost all water. Water efficiency is becoming a board-level priority for manufacturers.

From reactive repair to smart networks

Many water networks were managed reactively: meters were read by hand and leaks were fixed after they appeared. Utilities are now installing smart meters, sensors and control systems that detect problems early and reduce non-revenue water.

Where the growth is

Population growth, climate change and industrial demand are putting pressure on supplies. That drives investment in desalination in dry regions, water reuse, industrial treatment and digital tools that cut losses.

Who should be listed

Water and wastewater treatment technology companies, desalination specialists, membrane manufacturers, metering and sensor makers, and digital water software providers.

Frequently asked questions

What is water technology?

Water technology covers the equipment, materials and software used to source, treat, distribute, monitor and reuse water.

What is non-revenue water?

It is water that is produced but lost before reaching customers, mostly through leaks and metering errors.

Which markets invest most in desalination?

Water-scarce regions such as the Middle East, parts of Asia and Australia are among the major users of desalination.
