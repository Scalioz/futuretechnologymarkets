Specialty Chemicals & Process Technology

Specialty chemicals give products their performance, from coatings and adhesives to pharmaceutical ingredients and electronic materials. Future Markets Technology tracks the process technology, digital tools and green chemistry innovators modernising chemical production.

From trial and error to data-driven chemistry

Chemical R&D once depended on long cycles of lab experiments, and plants were controlled largely by hand. Distributed control systems and advanced process control are now standard. AI and high-throughput experimentation are the next step, helping chemists find better formulations faster.

Where the growth is

Customers want high-performance materials with a lower environmental footprint. That supports growth in bio-based chemicals, recycling of plastics into chemical feedstock, electronic chemicals for semiconductors, and battery materials.

Who should be listed

Specialty chemical producers with distinctive technology, process equipment and reactor makers, automation and software vendors, catalyst developers and green chemistry startups.

Frequently asked questions

What are specialty chemicals?

Specialty chemicals are produced for specific performance or function, such as coatings, adhesives, additives and electronic chemicals, rather than sold as bulk commodities.

What is green chemistry?

Green chemistry designs products and processes that reduce or eliminate hazardous substances, waste and energy use.

Is the portal open to chemical startups?

Yes. Startups with proven technology can apply and are verified like every other brand.
