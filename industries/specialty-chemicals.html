Specialty Chemicals & Process Technology

Specialty chemicals give products their performance, from coatings and adhesives to pharmaceutical ingredients and electronic materials. Future Markets Technology tracks the process technology, digital tools and green chemistry innovators modernising chemical production.

Key specialty chemicals technologies we cover

Advanced process controlModel-based control systems keep reactors, columns and other units at optimal conditions around the clock. Plants increase yield, reduce energy use and run more safely. Advanced process control builds on existing distributed control systems.
AI-assisted R&DMachine learning models analyse experimental data to suggest promising molecules and formulations. Combined with automated lab equipment, they shorten development cycles from years to months. Chemists spend more time on the most promising options.
Flow chemistryContinuous flow reactors carry out reactions in small channels with precise control of temperature and mixing. They are safer for hazardous reactions and easier to scale from lab to production. Flow chemistry is growing in fine chemicals and pharmaceutical ingredients.
Green & bio-based chemistryRenewable feedstocks, biotechnology and lower-impact processes replace some fossil-based chemicals. Producers reduce emissions and hazardous by-products. Customers in consumer goods and automotive increasingly ask for these alternatives.
Catalysis innovationNew catalysts make reactions faster, more selective and less energy-intensive. Better catalysis means fewer unwanted by-products and lower costs. Catalysts are also central to recycling plastics and producing low-carbon fuels.
Plant digital twinsA virtual model of the plant mirrors real operations using live sensor data. Engineers test changes, train operators and plan maintenance without risk to production. Digital twins support safer, more reliable operations.

From trial and error to data-driven chemistry

Chemical R&D once depended on long cycles of lab experiments, and plants were controlled largely by hand. Distributed control systems and advanced process control are now standard. AI and high-throughput experimentation are the next step, helping chemists find better formulations faster.

Where the growth is

Customers want high-performance materials with a lower environmental footprint. That supports growth in bio-based chemicals, recycling of plastics into chemical feedstock, electronic chemicals for semiconductors, and battery materials.

Who should be listed

Specialty chemical producers with distinctive technology, process equipment and reactor makers, automation and software vendors, catalyst developers and green chemistry startups.

Frequently asked questions

What are specialty chemicals?

Specialty chemicals are produced for specific performance or function, such as coatings, adhesives, additives and electronic chemicals, rather than sold as bulk commodities.

What is green chemistry?

Green chemistry designs products and processes that reduce or eliminate hazardous substances, waste and energy use.

Is the portal open to chemical startups?

Yes. Startups with proven technology can apply and are verified like every other brand.
