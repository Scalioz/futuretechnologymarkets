Mining Technology & Critical Minerals

Mining supplies the raw materials behind construction, electronics and the energy transition. Future Markets Technology showcases the mining equipment, technology and services (METS) companies making mines safer, more productive and more sustainable.

Key mining & minerals technologies we cover

Autonomous haulage & drillingDriverless haul trucks and automated drill rigs follow planned routes and patterns under supervision from control rooms. Automation keeps people away from hazardous areas and allows equipment to work more hours. Consistent operation also reduces fuel use and tyre wear.
Sensor-based ore sortingX-ray, laser and other sensors identify valuable ore and separate it from waste rock early in the process. Less waste goes to energy-intensive grinding and processing. Ore sorting saves energy and water and extends the life of a deposit.
Drones & surveyingDrones capture high-resolution images and 3D models of pits, stockpiles and tailings facilities in hours. Surveyors avoid dangerous terrain and planners work with up-to-date data. Regular drone surveys also support safety and environmental monitoring.
Mine digital twinsA digital twin combines geology, equipment and production data in one live virtual model of the mine. Planners can simulate schedules, test changes and spot bottlenecks before acting on site. Digital twins link planning with daily operations.
Electrification of mine fleetsBattery-electric loaders, trucks and trolley-assist systems reduce diesel use and emissions. Underground mines also benefit from less heat and better air quality, which cuts ventilation costs. Charging infrastructure and battery swapping are developing fast.
Critical minerals processingNew refining and separation technologies process lithium, nickel, cobalt, copper and rare earths more efficiently and with less environmental impact. Governments are supporting domestic processing capacity to secure supply chains. This is one of the fastest-growing areas in mining technology.

From manual drilling to remote operations

Traditional mining relied on operator-driven equipment and manual surveying. Leading operations now run autonomous haul trucks, use drones for daily surveys and manage entire sites from remote operations centres hundreds of kilometres away.

Where the growth is

Demand for copper, lithium, nickel and rare earths is rising with electrification and renewable energy. Governments are also prioritising secure supplies of critical minerals. This drives investment in exploration technology, processing innovation, water recycling and lower-emission mine fleets.

Who should be listed

Mining equipment manufacturers, automation and software providers, exploration technology companies, processing and refining technology developers, and environmental and water management specialists.

Frequently asked questions

What is mining technology?

Mining technology includes the equipment, automation, software and processing methods used to explore, extract and refine minerals.

What are critical minerals?

Critical minerals are raw materials considered essential for economic and national security, often including lithium, cobalt, nickel, copper, graphite and rare earth elements. Each government defines its own list.

Which countries lead in mining technology?

Countries with large mining sectors, including Australia, Canada and Sweden, are known for mining equipment, technology and services.
