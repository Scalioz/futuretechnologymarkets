Energy Technology & Energy Transition

The global energy system is going through its biggest change in a century. Future Markets Technology covers the companies behind renewable power, energy storage, smart grids and hydrogen, as well as the technology modernising conventional energy.

Battery energy storageGrid-scale batteries that balance variable renewable output.

Green hydrogenElectrolysers producing hydrogen from renewable electricity.
Energy efficiencyTechnologies that cut industrial and building energy use.
AI grid managementForecasting and balancing supply and demand in real time.

From one-way grids to intelligent systems

Power systems were built for large central plants sending electricity one way to consumers. Renewables, rooftop solar, batteries and electric vehicles are turning grids into complex, two-way networks that need digital control.

Where the growth is

Global investment in clean energy has grown strongly, with solar, wind, grids and storage attracting the largest shares. Industrial decarbonisation, hydrogen and grid modernisation are the next major investment waves.

Who should be listed

Renewable energy technology manufacturers, storage and battery companies, grid technology and software providers, hydrogen developers, and energy efficiency specialists.

Frequently asked questions

What is energy technology?

Energy technology includes the equipment, systems and software used to generate, store, transmit and use energy.

What is a smart grid?

A smart grid uses sensors, communications and automation to manage electricity flows more efficiently and integrate renewable energy.

Do you cover oil and gas technology?

Yes. Technology that improves efficiency, safety or emissions in conventional energy is covered alongside renewables.
