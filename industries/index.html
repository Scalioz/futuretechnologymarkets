Pharmaceutical Manufacturing & Technology

Pharmaceutical manufacturing is moving from batch processing and paper records to connected, data-driven plants. Future Markets Technology tracks the pharma technology companies, equipment makers and software providers that are making medicines faster, safer and more affordable to produce.

Healthcare & Medical Technology

Healthcare technology is changing how patients are diagnosed, treated and monitored. From AI-assisted imaging to connected medical devices and hospital-at-home programmes, Future Markets Technology brings together the medtech and digital health companies behind this change.

Textile & Apparel Manufacturing Technology

Textiles and apparel form one of the world's largest manufacturing industries and one of its biggest employers. Future Markets Technology showcases the textile machinery makers, software developers and sustainable materials innovators transforming how fabric and clothing are made.

Food Processing Technology

Food processing turns raw agricultural produce into safe, shelf-stable products for billions of people. Future Markets Technology highlights the equipment makers, automation specialists and food safety technology companies that are making food production cleaner, faster and more traceable.

Packaging Technology & Machinery

Packaging protects products, carries brands and moves goods through global supply chains. Future Markets Technology covers the packaging machinery makers, materials innovators and smart packaging developers shaping a more efficient and sustainable industry.

Mining Technology & Critical Minerals

Mining supplies the raw materials behind construction, electronics and the energy transition. Future Markets Technology showcases the mining equipment, technology and services (METS) companies making mines safer, more productive and more sustainable.

Specialty Chemicals & Process Technology

Specialty chemicals give products their performance, from coatings and adhesives to pharmaceutical ingredients and electronic materials. Future Markets Technology tracks the process technology, digital tools and green chemistry innovators modernising chemical production.

Water & Wastewater Technology

Water security is one of the defining challenges of this century. Future Markets Technology brings together the water treatment, desalination, reuse and digital water companies helping cities and industries do more with every drop.

Construction Technology (ConTech)

Construction is one of the world's largest industries and is now digitising fast. Future Markets Technology showcases the construction technology companies bringing BIM, prefabrication, robotics and low-carbon materials to building sites worldwide.

Energy Technology & Energy Transition

The global energy system is going through its biggest change in a century. Future Markets Technology covers the companies behind renewable power, energy storage, smart grids and hydrogen, as well as the technology modernising conventional energy.
