Construction Technology (ConTech)

Construction is one of the world's largest industries and is now digitising fast. Future Markets Technology showcases the construction technology companies bringing BIM, prefabrication, robotics and low-carbon materials to building sites worldwide.

Key construction technologies we cover

Building Information Modelling (BIM)BIM creates a shared, data-rich 3D model of a building or infrastructure project. Architects, engineers and contractors coordinate in one model and find clashes before construction starts. The model later supports facility management.
Modular & prefabricated constructionRooms, facades and building modules are manufactured in factories and assembled on site. Projects finish faster, with better quality control and less waste. Modular methods are growing in housing, hotels, hospitals and data centres.
Site roboticsRobots lay bricks, drill ceilings, tie rebar, mark layouts and inspect progress. They take on repetitive and physically demanding tasks and help address skills shortages. Robotic layout tools also improve accuracy.
Drones & reality captureDrones and laser scanners capture the site as it really is, and compare it with the design model. Project managers track progress, measure volumes and spot errors early. Regular scans create a reliable record of the project.
Construction 3D printingLarge printers build walls and structures layer by layer directly from digital models. Printing can reduce material waste and labour for certain building types. Projects range from houses to bridges and site structures.
Low-carbon materialsLow-clinker cement, recycled steel, mass timber and new insulation products reduce the carbon footprint of buildings. Developers and governments increasingly set embodied-carbon targets. Material innovation is a major growth area.

From paper drawings to digital twins

Construction long relied on 2D drawings and manual site work. BIM and drone surveys are now widely used on major projects. The next wave connects buildings to digital twins that support operations for decades after handover.

Where the growth is

Urbanisation, infrastructure programmes and housing demand keep construction volumes high. Productivity gaps and skills shortages push contractors toward prefabrication and automation, while climate targets drive demand for low-carbon materials and energy-efficient buildings.

Who should be listed

Construction software developers, equipment and robotics manufacturers, modular building companies, building materials innovators and smart building system providers.

Frequently asked questions

What is construction technology?

Construction technology, or ConTech, covers the software, machines, materials and methods used to design, build and operate buildings and infrastructure more efficiently.

What is BIM?

Building Information Modelling is a process that uses shared digital models to plan, design, construct and manage buildings and infrastructure.

Does the portal list contractors?
