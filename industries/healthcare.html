Healthcare & Medical Technology

Healthcare technology is changing how patients are diagnosed, treated and monitored. From AI-assisted imaging to connected medical devices and hospital-at-home programmes, Future Markets Technology brings together the medtech and digital health companies behind this change.

Point-of-care testingRapid diagnostic devices used at the bedside or in the community.

From hospital walls to everywhere

Care used to happen almost entirely inside hospitals, with paper files and film-based imaging. Digital records, digital imaging and telehealth have already changed daily practice. The next stage brings AI into diagnosis, moves more care into the home and uses data to predict which patients need attention first.

Where the growth is

Health systems worldwide face ageing populations and staff shortages. That creates strong demand for technology that saves clinician time, reduces readmissions and extends access to care. Medical imaging, remote patient monitoring, clinical AI, hospital automation and cybersecurity for connected devices are among the fastest-moving areas.

Who should be listed

Medical device manufacturers, healthcare software and AI developers, diagnostic and imaging companies, hospital infrastructure and automation suppliers, and digital health platforms with proven deployments.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as healthcare technology?

Healthcare technology covers medical devices, diagnostic equipment, health software, AI tools, telemedicine and hospital systems used to prevent, diagnose, treat or monitor disease.
