Healthcare & Medical Technology

Healthcare technology is changing how patients are diagnosed, treated and monitored. From AI-assisted imaging to connected medical devices and hospital-at-home programmes, Future Markets Technology brings together the medtech and digital health companies behind this change.

Key healthcare technologies we cover

AI-assisted diagnosticsAlgorithms trained on large image datasets help radiologists and pathologists spot findings in X-rays, CT, MRI and digital slides. They can prioritise urgent cases and reduce reading time. Clinicians stay in control, using AI as a second pair of eyes.
Connected medical devicesWearables, home monitors and implantable devices send vital signs and device data to care teams. Remote monitoring helps detect deterioration early and reduces hospital readmissions. Secure connectivity and data standards are essential for safe deployment.
Telemedicine platformsVideo consultations, remote triage and e-prescribing bring specialist care to patients who live far from hospitals. Telemedicine reduces waiting times and travel costs. It is now a permanent part of care delivery in many health systems.
Surgical roboticsRobot-assisted systems give surgeons greater precision, steadier instruments and better visualisation. Minimally invasive procedures can mean smaller incisions, less pain and shorter hospital stays. New systems are becoming smaller and more affordable.
Electronic health recordsInteroperable digital records connect hospitals, laboratories, pharmacies and clinics. Clinicians see a complete patient history in one place, which reduces duplicate tests and medication errors. Health data also supports research and population health planning.
Point-of-care testingPortable analysers and rapid tests deliver results at the bedside, in clinics or in the community within minutes. Faster results mean faster treatment decisions. Point-of-care testing is especially valuable in emergency care and remote regions.

From hospital walls to everywhere

Care used to happen almost entirely inside hospitals, with paper files and film-based imaging. Digital records, digital imaging and telehealth have already changed daily practice. The next stage brings AI into diagnosis, moves more care into the home and uses data to predict which patients need attention first.

Where the growth is

Health systems worldwide face ageing populations and staff shortages. That creates strong demand for technology that saves clinician time, reduces readmissions and extends access to care. Medical imaging, remote patient monitoring, clinical AI, hospital automation and cybersecurity for connected devices are among the fastest-moving areas.

Who should be listed

Medical device manufacturers, healthcare software and AI developers, diagnostic and imaging companies, hospital infrastructure and automation suppliers, and digital health platforms with proven deployments.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as healthcare technology?

Healthcare technology covers medical devices, diagnostic equipment, health software, AI tools, telemedicine and hospital systems used to prevent, diagnose, treat or monitor disease.
